PHOTOS AND PICTURES

Your photo:
  Upload it here named  photo.jpg  (photo.jpeg or photo.png also work).
  To replace it, upload a new file with the same name.
  A portrait-shaped photo works best. It's cropped to fit automatically.
  Until a photo is here, the site shows your initials.

Project pictures (optional):
  Upload them here, then add the file name to the project's "image"
  line in content/projects.js, for example:  image: "images/saathi.png",
